Selected Work
Axiom Zen · All the Zen · 2025

Brought decentralized token utility to life through gaming.

Collaborated with CryptoKitties on All the Zen, a two-week decentralized gaming event set within the CryptoKitties multiverse, giving utility to the community's tokens through gaming and interactive experiences. I led design on the slots game experience and helped manage the team, giving over 2,500 participants a live, functional way to put their tokens to use. The event was well received, validating the concept even though it wasn't taken further afterward.

Axiom Zen · Toby · 2024

Grew paid adoption ~10x with a pricing redesign.

Toby's pricing model was converting few free users into paid ones. I led the pricing and paywall redesign from research through launch, restructuring the plan tiers and rebuilding the upgrade flow around real usage patterns we uncovered in research. The result: ~10x growth in paid adoption and ~12x revenue growth within a year.

Axiom Zen · Toby · 2023

Three AI features that eliminated manual tab work.

Reduced manual tab organizing with three AI-assisted features. I led design on Generate title, Sort tab, and Group tabs, each removing a manual step from organizing browser tabs and collections. All three shipped and noticeably reduced the time and effort users spent organizing.

Dapper Labs · NBA Top Shot · 2019

Pioneered research that shaped both product and visual direction for an emerging marketplace.

NBA Top Shot was entering an emerging space with no existing playbook for crypto-native collectibles. I pioneered early user research, building personas and leading interviews that tested early product concepts. The findings surfaced valuable feedback that informed the broader product conversation, and directly shaped key visual design decisions for the marketplace.
